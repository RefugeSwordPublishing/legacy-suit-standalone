# Discovery questionnaire — crew-clock

Run this with the contractor before any building. Starred (★) items most change
the build; don't let those stay vague. Frame him as pilot customer #1 and design
partner, so the product-lens questions in section K matter as much as the features.

## A. Business snapshot
- What does the company do, and how many people total?
- How many are in the field (clocking time) vs office?
- How are hours tracked today (paper, texts, spreadsheet, an app)? What breaks about it? ★
- The one thing that, if the app nailed it, makes this worth it? ★

## B. People and roles (who operates it)
- Who logs in: owner, office/admin, foremen/site managers, crew, subs, clients?
- Who sees everyone's data vs only their own?
- Who approves timecards and corrections? ★
- Own phones, or a shared tablet at the trailer/shop (kiosk)? ★

## C. Time clocking (the core — go deep)
- Clock in once a day, or switch between multiple jobs in a day? ★
- Clock against a job/project? Against a task or cost code? ★
- GPS location capture or geofencing to confirm on-site? Clock-in photo? ★
- Breaks: paid or unpaid, manual or auto-deducted?
- Overtime rules: daily over 8, weekly over 40, doubletime, state-specific? Which state(s)? ★
- Time rounding (nearest 15 min, or exact)?
- Forgot-to-clock-out handling: auto-clock-out at a set time? ★
- Do crews work with no cell signal? Must clocking work offline and sync later? ★
- Where do approved hours go: payroll system, QuickBooks, spreadsheet, PDF? ★

## D. Projects / job tracking
- Full project management, or just enough to tag hours to a job?
- Fields per job: client, address, budget, budgeted hours, status/phase, dates?
- Assign crew to specific jobs?
- Tasks/checklists per job, or not needed for v1?

## E. Materials
- Track per job (needed → ordered → delivered), or just a running list?
- Who adds/updates (field or office)?
- Tie to cost/budget, or just a to-buy list for v1?

## F. Expenses
- Track job expenses? Receipt photos? Categories (materials, subs, fuel)?
- Roll into job cost / billing, or just recorded?

## G. The money side (decide what's in scope for v1)
- Needs estimates/invoices/client billing in-app, or staying in QuickBooks/elsewhere? ★
- Any QuickBooks connection wanted (customers, jobs, invoices, payroll)?

## H. Devices and access
- iPhone, Android, or both in the crew? ★
- A real installed app (App/Play Store), or is opening a link on the phone fine? ★
- Office use on laptop/desktop too?

## I. Integrations and data out
- Payroll provider (Gusto, ADP, QuickBooks Payroll, other)?
- Day-one exports wanted (weekly hours CSV, per-job cost)?

## J. Rules and rollout
- Labor-compliance rules he's legally on the hook for (break laws, OT, sick time)? ★
- How soon does he want something usable, and for how many people at first?
- Who maintains it after launch, and is he paying for the build and/or support? ★★

## K. Product lens (because he's pilot customer #1)
- What does he pay for software today (time tracking, PM, anything), and how much? ★
- Would he be a reference customer and give feedback through the build?
- If this saved him X hours a week, what would that be worth per month? ★
- What would make him switch off whatever he uses now, and what would make him not?
